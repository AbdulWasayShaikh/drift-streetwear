# DRIFT, monochrome streetwear

A demo landing page for an invented streetwear brand, designed and built by Abdul Wasay Shaikh.

- Plain HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, with GSAP, ScrollTrigger and Lenis.
- Every section flips in on scroll; a product showcase, a card rail, a bento grid, a marquee and a working bag.
- The hero and the main products use AI-generated photos made in Google Flow; the other slots show drawn garments until photos are listed in `assets/img/images.json`.

The brand, prices, stats and reviews are invented for the demo, and the photos are AI-generated. The newsletter and checkout send nothing.
